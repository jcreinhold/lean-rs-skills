# On Mathematical Exposition

State the main claim in plain English before adding notation. Introduce each symbol when it first matters and explain what it denotes. Use words where notation adds no precision.

Tell the reader why each step comes next, not only that it is valid. State edge cases and conventions. Keep the tone plain; rigor comes from the argument, not formal-sounding prose. A good final pass asks whether a reader can identify the claim, objects, assumptions, and role of each lemma without guessing.
